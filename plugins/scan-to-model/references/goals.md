# Reconstruction goals

Goals describe the intended project outcome and its limits. Keep the goal in the private project record; this reference gives a reusable shape and records the current project goal as an example. It does not define a goal command, runtime or API.

## Reusable goal shape

```text
Outcome: <what should be reviewable at the end>
Scope: <supported geometry, contents, evidence, drawings and handoff included>
Order: <large structural dependencies before smaller details; stable regions may proceed independently>
Parallel work: <historical or evidence work that can continue while another decision is held>
Preservation: <accepted geometry, source records and prior limits that must survive>
Completion evidence: <model, stills, drawings, register and review records required>
Exclusions: <explicitly excluded deliverables or external assets>
Decision rule: <what remains provisional, held or unknown, and who resolves conflicts>
```

Keep investigation completion separate from physical completion. A bounded source review can finish with an unresolved identity or measurement while another supported region continues. Record the exact next decision, evidence or review needed rather than converting a partial result into project completion.

## Current project goal example

> Complete the existing house-mapping plan, excluding movie work and externally owned backups.
>
> Work from large structure inward: alignment and levels, supported enclosure, room connections, fixed assemblies and contents, fine details, then coordinated drawings and final verification. Advance stable regions independently and review historical construction in parallel.
>
> Use Luna agents with the reusable role prompts, retaining root judgment and acceptance review. Extract proven reusable tools, skills and prompts into the plugin; validate, commit and push directly.
>
> Preserve accepted geometry and keep unsupported areas explicit. Track physical completion separately from completed investigations. Deliver reviewed model increments, occasional screenshots, drawings and an accurate final disposition of the full task register.
>
> Continue until the authorized work is complete. If a necessary measurement or decision blocks progress, identify the exact need and continue independent work.

In this example, Luna agents, direct publishing, movie exclusion and externally owned backups are project choices rather than universal plugin defaults. Private source paths, addresses, transforms and owner observations remain in private projects.
