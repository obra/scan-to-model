# Evidence workflow

Preserve originals and add new sources with new IDs. Link each model claim to its supporting capture, frame or photo, pixel selection and variant through the project's [source manifest](project-records.md#source-manifest). Label claims as observed, measured, inferred, owner supplied, historical or unknown.

The coverage atlas tracks rooms, surfaces, and connections as supported, partial, conflicting, inaccessible, or unobserved. Shot lists point to actual source photos; retire requests once evidence exists. A ruler is optional independent control. For execution and delegation, use the [model selection guidance](work-packets.md#model-selection).

Depth availability, local repeatability, and absolute accuracy are separate findings. Glass may return background; furniture may occlude fixed surfaces. Compare dated open-wall evidence locally with current scans before carrying service routes forward. Current scans normally determine current finishes.

Use rigid shared surfaces and distributed landmarks. Keep withheld check sets independent. Preserve rejected samples and all point residuals. Regularize measured geometry with planes or right angles only when evidence supports it; never deform rooms to hide drift. Missing evidence remains unknown even when the requested model needs a visible surface. If the owner authorized interpolation, make a reasoned choice and mark the affected model geometry or appearance tentative, with its basis and unresolved dimensions. That permission does not establish measured contacts, accepted registration or observed concealed services. Without it, leave unsupported boundaries unresolved.

In Blender, separate architecture, contents, current, historical, and services. Preserve unrelated objects and materials. Review source matched and top/section renders and reopen saved files. A reviewed deliverable may include authorized tentative choices; keep them distinguishable from evidence-supported geometry after delivery. Neither a tentative label nor a polished render excuses a known contradiction with the sources.
