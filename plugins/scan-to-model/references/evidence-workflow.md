# Evidence workflow

Keep an immutable source ledger with SHA256 hashes. Derived points, planes, transforms and objects link to their archive/capture, frame or photo, relevant native coordinates, variant and provenance class: observed, measured, inferred, owner supplied, historical or unknown. Reuse these links through subsequent stages; each handoff does not need to copy the same evidence again.

## Evidence sufficient for the current build

Carry identity, metric accuracy, placement and physical-contact acceptance separately. Read the latest scoped disposition before a predecessor report. An accepted identity remains accepted when another dimension is unknown. A project-defined seed frame can support the qualified use explicitly granted to it; it does not become independently validated registration. Apply source-to-model coordinates once and preserve the source scope and accuracy limits.

Inspect original images for the physical feature being modeled. Contact sheets locate sources; rotation checks, hashes and reviewed PDF pages do not establish full-resolution photograph inspection. The coverage atlas records supported, partial, conflicting, inaccessible or unobserved surfaces and connections. Update it when a finding changes coverage or the next action, and retire capture requests once their evidence exists.

For a new measurement, name the physical fact, the sources to inspect and the decision it will change. Stop checking when the available evidence supports the declared geometry with stated limits, or when those sources cannot resolve the question. Additional views are useful when they expose a missing face or discriminate a particular conflict. A source-local fit that cannot resolve the required placement is a finite diagnostic; preserve it and advance independent geometry.

Depth availability, local repeatability and absolute accuracy are separate findings. Glass may return background, and furniture may occlude fixed surfaces. Use rigid shared surfaces and distributed landmarks for registration, with independent withheld checks. Preserve rejected samples and residuals. Regularize planes or right angles only with physical support; never deform rooms to conceal drift. Unknown boundaries remain unknown.

Current scans normally determine current finishes. Reconcile dated open-wall evidence locally before carrying observed service routes forward. Historical placement, current survival and hidden continuity are separate claims.

## Retain once, bind at execution

Preserve source bytes at intake and retain the exact reviewed selections. An unchanged retained artifact can be referenced by path and hash by multiple workers and runs. Copy it only when needed to prevent mutation or to satisfy an execution helper's input contract. Do not re-ingest sources, rebuild complete inventories, or create a second provenance chain for a read-only lookup.

Keep draft notes and unexecuted preparation within the active increment. Before executing a producer whose output will be retained as evidence, freeze its exact bytes and bind its inputs using the maintained runner. Keep the runner's receipt, runtime and outputs together, including failed attempts. Fresh output paths protect executed evidence from overwrite; they are not a requirement to create a new work packet for every question, correction or handoff. A corrected execution gets its own run directory inside the same increment. Do not rerun an unchanged accepted producer merely to give a successor packet its own receipt.

Native Blender runs retain their stricter model/helper identity, runtime and preservation requirements in [blender-review.md](blender-review.md). Use the existing runners rather than duplicating their freezing and manifest work by hand. Historical artifacts remain unchanged; current summaries link to the latest applicable disposition.

## Review and closeout

Separate architecture, contents, current finishes, historical construction and services in Blender. Review the changed geometry and adjoining interfaces against the sources, inspect the saved candidate, and compare the declared change against the baseline inventory. Choose views that expose shape, openings, contact, orientation and preservation risks. Do not expand a local review into a whole-house recertification without a new shared dependency or global change.

Write one closeout for the coherent increment: what was built or prepared, source/transform bindings, review result, remaining physical limits and next action. Update the existing ledger, package and affected delivery links together; refer to tool receipts instead of transcribing them. Record an unexpected conflict or stop immediately so another worker cannot consume invalid state. Routine counts and summaries can wait until closeout.

## Multi-view corner rays

Review full-resolution pixels and confirm that every view selects the same physical corner; a bevel or underside corner is a different feature. Inspect pair angles, conditioning, signed along-ray distances, perpendicular residuals and reprojection errors. Correlated pose or pixel sensitivity does not establish field accuracy, and a source-local point does not determine a whole component.
